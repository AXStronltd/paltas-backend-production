### Work orders

Root causes: no stable get-one/correction endpoint or detail form; no optimistic version; sending completed work back cleared its current completion fields without an immutable before snapshot. Added repeatable pre-quotation scope editing and unfinished-work priority/due-date editing with reason, current-record retrieval, paginated scoped audit history and database versioning. Quoted scope is preserved; completed/billed/cancelled work cannot be silently rewritten. Quality rejection now snapshots the completion evidence and notes. Existing requests can be reopened for follow-up work without replacing bills or prior orders.

Path: Property Management → Operations → Work orders and inspections → Open work order.

### Property preventive servicing

Root cause: Hotel already had preventive automation, but Property Management had no corresponding persisted customer-configured servicing schedule connected to its existing work orders. Added property/unit servicing plans, optional equipment/facility label, repeat interval, next due date, pause/resume, repeated edits and history. Reused the durable core scheduler and extracted the existing shared work-order creation transaction helper. No new purchasing or financial engine.

An active due plan creates one unapproved work order, retains an immutable occurrence and advances its next due date atomically. Existing unfinished servicing prevents overlapping generation. Missed periods produce one overdue service, not automatic historical backlogs. Retries/old recurring periods cannot duplicate an occurrence. Every run checks current subscription, organisation availability, author membership/capability and delegated property scope. Batches process at most 100 due plans, rotating checks to prevent blocked plans starving later work. No invoice, supplier approval, email or payment is generated by plan setup.

Path: Property Management → Operations → Work orders and inspections → Preventive schedules.

## Full enterprise specification remains open

This release is not enterprise-wide completion. The requirement-level People & Leasing completion gate still fails, intentionally and transparently, because acceptance evidence and implementation remain open. The 105-plan test is not certification for 100,000 units. Broader ownership shares/co-tenants, full application/signing/screening and handover workflows, all-product inventory/assets/procurement/safety/projects, full finance/migration/reporting acceptance, country configuration, recovery exercises, performance and customer acceptance remain open. Banking and M-Pesa are disconnected and are not the only remaining work. External signature, identity, hardware and jurisdiction rules need actual supported configuration and operational validation.

The matrices retain open items; no incomplete feature was marked enterprise-complete to release these separately authorised, tested operational batches.
