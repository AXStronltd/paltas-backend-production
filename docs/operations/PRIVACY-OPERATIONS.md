# Privacy operations for the production release

The customer-facing Terms, Privacy Notice and Data Processing Agreement live in the frontend `legal/` directory and enter the production allowlist. They must not be substituted with the staging evaluation agreement.

Contact: supportpaltas@gmail.com. The operator must monitor this inbox for privacy requests and security incidents. Do not request passwords, authenticator codes, banking credentials or bulk tenant documents by email.

For each request, record receipt, the claimed company/user, the requested action and responsible operator in a restricted case record. Verify authority through the registered account and proportional checks; company administrators do not automatically control unrelated people's records. Identify whether AXSTRON is controller or processor for the requested information. For customer-controlled workspace records, obtain the controller's documented instructions and assist it.

Assess applicable response deadlines, legal holds, financial retention, third-party rights and backup expiry. Provide the applicable response without claiming that a request is automatically completed. Export or deletion must be scoped to the verified company/person, reviewed and independently checked. Do not run generic database deletion commands. Record completion and exceptions; reapply erasure instructions after any later restoration.

For a suspected breach, preserve evidence and record the known time, affected companies, record categories, likely consequences and containment steps. Notify the affected controller without undue delay and update it as information becomes available. Assess applicable regulator/individual notification requirements separately; do not assume every incident has the same notification duty.

Before adding a provider or changing processing locations, assess the data shared, purpose, security, provider DPA, applicable transfer mechanism and required customer notice/objection period. Public provider documents reviewed for this release: Render https://render.com/dpa; Netlify https://www.netlify.com/gdpr-ccpa/; Resend https://resend.com/legal/dpa. These documents do not by themselves prove that a case-specific transfer assessment or operator procedure has been executed.
